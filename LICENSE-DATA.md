## Covered by CC BY 4.0

- `model/**`: everything a build writes there (IFC, `.blend`, glTF / GLB, engine and manifest JSON, walk-test
  reports and maps, plans and sections).
- `reports/**`: the renders and every other artefact a build or `estate report` writes there (`report.html`,
  `report.json`, `issues.bcf`, the site plans, the schedules and the JSON reports).
- The model and reports zips attached to this repository's GitHub releases.
- The single-block baseline at the root: `hdb_block.ifc`, `hdb_block_ifc4.ifc`, `hdb_block.glb`,
  `hdb_block_engine.json`, and its drawings `block_views.png`, `plan_L5.png`, `stair_section.png` and
  `nav_L5_compare.png`.

## Not covered (MIT, see `LICENSE`)

  `export_engine.py`) and their README.
- `legacy/**`: the same toolkit, unpacked.
- Everything else in the repository: `estate/**`, `config/**`, `tests/**`, `estate.py`, `estate.sh`, `estate.cmd`
  and the documentation.
